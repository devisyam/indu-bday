A few words for the birthday girl, written from the place where all my best thoughts begin: you.

27 September 2026with love, always

My beautiful Indira,

Happy birthday to the person who makes my life feel more like itself.

This is your first birthday after our engagement, and I keep catching myself smiling at the thought that I get to call you my fiancée. I have loved you in so many seasons already, but loving you with forever in our plans feels like a new kind of happiness.

I hope this next year brings you the kind of happiness that stays after the candles are gone—the quiet, certain kind that lives in your chest and reminds you that you are loved beyond measure.

Thank you for your softness, your strength, your laugh, and all the tiny ways you make a day better without even trying. I still get to call you my girlfriend, my fiancée, and my favourite person in the same lifetime. I don’t think I’ll ever stop feeling lucky about that.

When I look at the little girl in the first photo and then at the woman I am going to marry, I feel grateful for every road that brought you to me. Here’s to every place we haven’t seen, every meal we haven’t shared, and every ordinary Tuesday that will become a memory because it happened with you.

I love you, endlessly.
Your almost-husband ♥
